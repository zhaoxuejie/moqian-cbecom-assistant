# Refunds — How They Work

> This page explains the refund **process** after an approved return. Refund requests are
> reviewed and approved by our support team; the assistant cannot approve or promise refunds.

## When a refund is issued

A refund is issued after we receive and inspect the returned item. We check that the item is
unused and in its original packaging, and that the return was authorised within the return
window for your country (see the Returns & Refunds Policy).

## Timing

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Return in transit to our warehouse | depends on carrier (usually 3–7 business days) |
| Inspection after delivery | 1–2 business days |
| Refund issued to the original payment method | **5–7 business days** after inspection |
| Bank or card issuer posting the refund | up to 10 business days, depending on the bank |

## Amount

- The item price is refunded in full for approved returns;
- Original shipping fees are refunded only when the return is due to our error (wrong item,
  defective item, or a shipping error);
- Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the return is due to our error;
- Discount codes are not refunded in cash and are not reissued after an order is returned.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new
order for the replacement; this keeps stock and timing accurate.

## If something looks wrong

If the refund does not appear after 10 business days, or the amount differs from what was
confirmed, contact **support@wanderbrim.com** with the order number and the refund date.
A human agent will investigate with the payment provider.
