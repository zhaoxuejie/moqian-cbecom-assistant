# Returns & Refunds Policy

> Effective March 1, 2026. **This is the current policy.** For orders shipped before March 1, 2026, the return window at the time of purchase applies.

## Return windows by country

You may return unused, unworn items within the window below, counted from the delivery date:

| Country | Return window | Who pays return shipping? |
|---|---|---|
| United States | **30 days** | Customer pays return shipping ($4.99 label) |
| United Kingdom | **14 days** (statutory right of withdrawal) | Customer pays return shipping |
| Germany | **14 days** (statutory Widerrufsrecht) | Customer pays return shipping (we provide a label for €4.99) |
| France | **14 days** (délai de rétractation) | Customer pays return shipping |
| Spain | **14 days** (desistimiento) | Customer pays return shipping |

> Note: Until February 28, 2026, the US return window was 60 days. That was changed to 30 days to match our product cost structure. Orders delivered on or after March 1, 2026 follow the 30-day window.

## Condition of returned items

Items must be:

- Unworn, unwashed, undamaged
- With all original tags attached
- In the original packaging

We cannot accept returns on items that show signs of wear, sun cream, sweat, or animal hair.

## Non-returnable items

- Hat clips, storage pouches, and other accessories (WB-ACC-005) are **final sale** once opened.
- Customized or embroidered hats are final sale.
- Items returned after the window has expired.

## How to return an item

1. Email **support@wanderbrim.com** with your order number and the reason for return.
2. We will reply with a **Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number** and a return shipping label.
3. Pack the item, write the RMA number on the outside of the box, and ship it to the warehouse for your region:
   - **US orders:** Wanderbrim Returns, 4800 S Airport Way, Phoenix, AZ 85034
   - **UK orders:** Wanderbrim Returns, Unit 12, Northenden Trading Estate, Manchester M22 4LN
   - **EU orders (DE/FR/ES):** Wanderbrim Returns, Lagerstraße 4, 04103 Leipzig, Germany

**Do not send items back without an RMA number.** Packages without an RMA cannot be matched to your order and may be refused.

## Refunds

- Once we receive and inspect the return, refunds are processed within **5–7 business days**.
- Refunds go to the **original payment method**. We cannot refund to a different card or account.
- Original shipping costs are non-refundable.
- The return shipping fee (if a label was provided) is deducted from the refund.

## Exchanges

We prefer to process exchanges as a return + reorder, so that you get the new size/color faster and we avoid shipping fees twice. If you prefer, we can instead send a replacement first — ask us in your return email.

## Damaged or wrong item

If we sent the wrong item, or your item arrived defective: we cover **both** the return shipping and a free replacement. Send photos within 7 days of delivery.
