# Promotions & Discount Terms

## Discount codes

### Welcome discount

New customers get **10% off their first order** with the code **WANDER10** at checkout. One use per customer.

### Free shipping thresholds

- US: free USPS shipping on orders **$50 and over**
- UK: free standard shipping on orders **£60 and over**
- EU (DE/FR/ES): free DPD shipping on orders **€75 and over**

These thresholds are calculated on the subtotal **after** any discount code.

### Bundle deals

Buy any two sun hats (WB-SUN-001 / WB-FIS-003) and get 15% off automatically at checkout — no code needed.

## General promo rules

- Only **one discount code per order**.
- Discount codes cannot be combined with the bundle deal.
- Discounts apply to merchandise only, not shipping.
- Accessories (WB-ACC-005) are excluded from all percentage discounts.
- Promo codes have an expiration date shown when the code is published; expired codes will not work.

## Price adjustments

We do not price-adjust orders placed before a sale. If an item goes on sale within 48 hours of your purchase, email us — we may issue a one-time discount code for the difference, at our discretion.

## Abandoned cart offers

If you leave items in your cart without checking out, we may email you a one-time discount code within 24 hours. These codes are personalized and can only be used once. If you don't receive one, it means there are no active offers for your cart.
