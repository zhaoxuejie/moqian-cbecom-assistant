# Contact Us & Support Hours

## Email

**support@wanderbrim.com** — we reply within **24 hours on business days**.

## Live chat

Live chat appears in the bottom-right corner of our website. An automated assistant answers common questions around the clock; a human agent takes over during support hours.

## Support hours

| Time zone | Hours (Mon–Fri) |
|---|---|
| US Mountain Time (MST/MDT) | 9:00 – 17:00 |
| US Eastern (EST/EDT) | 11:00 – 19:00 |
| Central European (CET/CEST) | 17:00 – 01:00 (next day) |
| UK (GMT/BST) | 16:00 – 00:00 (next day) |

We are closed on **US federal holidays**. Outside these hours, the assistant will answer what it can and leave a note for a human to follow up the next business day.

## What to include in your message

To help us resolve your issue faster, please include:

- Your **order number** (it starts with `WB-` and is in your confirmation email)
- The product SKU (e.g. WB-SUN-001)
- What you expected vs. what happened
- Photos, if the item arrived damaged

Without an order number, we can still answer policy questions, but we **cannot look up a specific order** for you.

## Languages

The live assistant understands English, Spanish, French, and German. Replies are generated in your language; our written policies are always written in English first and translated for reference.
