# Wholesale & B2B Enquiries

> This page covers bulk and reseller enquiries. It is a **reference document**, not an
> online ordering channel: wholesale orders are quoted and confirmed by our team.

## Who we work with

We supply independent retailers, outdoor stores, hotels and promotional resellers.
We do not run a dropshipping programme and we do not sell through marketplaces.

## Minimum order

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum order quantity (MOQ) | 100 units per style |
| Assorted colours | Allowed within one style, in multiples of 25 |
| Sample orders | Up to 3 units per style at retail price, credited against a later bulk order |

## Pricing and lead time

- Wholesale pricing starts at 45% off the retail price and is quoted per order;
- Standard lead time is **10–15 business days** after the order is confirmed, plus shipping;
- Custom embroidery or logo printing adds **5 business days** and is quoted separately;
- Peak season (October–December) lead times can extend by up to 10 business days.

## How to request a quote

Email **wholesale@wanderbrim.com** with: company name, destination country, styles and
quantities, and your requested delivery date. Our team replies within two business days.

Wholesale orders are invoiced directly; they are not eligible for consumer promotions or
retail discount codes.

## Returns on wholesale orders

Wholesale orders follow the same defect policy as retail orders, but change-of-mind returns
are not accepted on bulk orders. Report any shortage or defect within 7 days of delivery.
