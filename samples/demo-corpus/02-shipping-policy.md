# Shipping Policy

> Effective March 1, 2026. This supersedes any shipping terms quoted before that date.

## Order processing time

Orders are processed within **1–2 business days**. Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding US federal holidays. Orders placed on weekends or holidays ship the next business day.

Tracking information is emailed to you as soon as the label is created — this can be up to 24 hours before the carrier actually scans the package.

## Shipping methods & delivery times

### United States (shipping from Phoenix, AZ)

| Method | Cost | Estimated delivery |
|---|---|---|
| USPS Ground Advantage | Free on orders ≥ $50; otherwise $4.99 | 5–8 business days |
| UPS Ground | $8.99 | 3–5 business days |
| UPS 2nd Day Air | $18.99 | 2 business days |

We do not ship to P.O. Boxes, APO/FPO, or US territories (including Puerto Rico, Guam) at this time.

### United Kingdom (shipping from Leipzig, DE to UK)

| Method | Cost | Estimated delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Standard (Royal Mail / DPD) | Free on orders ≥ £60; otherwise £4.99 | 5–7 business days |
| Express (DHL Express) | £12.99 | 2–4 business days |

### European Union — Germany, France, Spain (shipping from Leipzig, DE)

| Method | Cost | Estimated delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Standard (DPD) | Free on orders ≥ €75; otherwise €5.99 | 5–7 business days (DE), 6–8 (FR/ES) |
| Express (DHL Express) | €14.99 | 2–4 business days |

Prices above include VAT. We ship DDP (delivered duty paid) for EU and UK orders, so **no extra customs charges should be due on delivery**.

## Customs, duties, and taxes

- **EU / UK:** Orders ship DDP. If you are asked for a customs fee on delivery, do not pay it — contact us first, because it means the shipment was misrouted.
- **US:** There are no import duties on orders under $800 USD.

## What if my tracking stops updating?

- Standard shipping tracking usually updates every **2–4 days**. A gap of 3–5 days is normal, especially between the origin country and your destination.
- If tracking has not moved for **more than 10 calendar days**, or shows "stuck in customs," contact us with your order number — we will open a trace with the carrier.
- If a package is not delivered within **30 days** of shipment, we treat it as lost (see below).

## Lost, delayed, or damaged in transit

- **Lost package (no update for 30 days, or carrier confirms loss):** We reship free of charge, or refund in full — your choice.
- **Delayed but moving:** We do not refund for delays caused by weather, customs, or carrier backlogs. For a delay over 14 business days beyond the estimate, we may offer a one-time discount code for a future order.
- **Damaged in transit:** Email photos of the item and the outer packaging within 7 days of delivery. We will reship or refund.

## Wrong address

We ship to the address you entered at checkout. If you contact us **before the order ships**, we can change the address for free. Once the order has shipped, address changes are at the carrier's discretion — we will try to intercept, but we cannot guarantee it. If the package is returned to us as undeliverable, we will refund the order minus the original shipping cost.
