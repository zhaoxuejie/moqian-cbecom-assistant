# About Wanderbrim

Wanderbrim Headwear makes packable, UPF 50+ sun hats for hikers, travelers, and everyday explorers. We started in 2023 after two years of testing prototypes on trails in the American Southwest.

## Our promises

- Every sun hat is rated **UPF 50+**, blocking 98% of UV rays.
- Packable: rolls into the included storage pouch without losing its shape.
- Designed in Arizona; shipped from warehouses in Phoenix (US) and Leipzig (EU).

## Where we ship

We currently ship to the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, and Spain. Other countries are not yet supported — if you write to us from elsewhere, we will explain that we cannot ship to you yet.

We are a **direct-to-consumer (DTC) brand**. We do not sell through Amazon, and our products are not available in retail stores.
