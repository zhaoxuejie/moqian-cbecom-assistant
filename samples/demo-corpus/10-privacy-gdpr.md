# Privacy & GDPR Basics

## What data we collect

- Contact details: name, email, shipping address, phone (if provided)
- Order history and payment confirmation (we never see your full card number)
- Chat and email support history

## What we use it for

- Processing and delivering your order
- Sending order confirmations and shipping updates
- Responding to support questions
- Email marketing **only if you opted in**

## Your rights (EU/UK customers)

Under GDPR and the UK GDPR, you can:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate data.
- **Request deletion** ("right to be forgotten") — we will delete data we are not required to keep for accounting or shipping records.
- **Object** to marketing emails — click the unsubscribe link in any email.
- **Data portability** — request a copy in a common format.

To exercise these rights, email **privacy@wanderbrim.com**. We respond within 30 days.

## Marketing emails

- We send order and shipping emails **transactionally** — you can't opt out of those.
- Promotional emails are sent only after you opt in. Unsubscribe at any time; one click and you're off the list.
- We **do not sell** your data to third parties.

## Data retention

- Order records are kept for 7 years for accounting and tax reasons.
- Support chat history is kept for 24 months, then anonymized.

## Children

Our site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
