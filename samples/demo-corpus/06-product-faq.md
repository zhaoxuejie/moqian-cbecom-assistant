# Product FAQ

## Materials and sun protection

### What does UPF 50+ mean?

UPF (Ultraviolet Protection Factor) 50+ means the fabric blocks at least **98% of UV rays**. All Wanderbrim sun hats (WB-SUN-001, WB-FIS-003) are rated UPF 50+. The baseball cap and beanie are not UPF-rated — they are for shade and warmth, not full sun protection.

### Are the hats waterproof?

The sun hat and bucket hat are **water-resistant**, not fully waterproof. They handle rain and splashes, but you should not submerge them or wear them in heavy downpours for long periods.

### How do I wash them?

- Hand wash in cold water with mild soap.
- Do not machine wash, tumble dry, or dry clean.
- Reshape while damp and air dry away from direct heat.
- Do not use bleach or fabric softener.

### Will packing it crush it?

The sun hat rolls into the included storage pouch and springs back. If it loses its shape after unpacking, steam it lightly and reshape — the wire brim holds its form.

## Use and fit

### Are the hats suitable for children?

Our sizes start at 54 cm, which fits most teenagers and adults. They are **not designed for young children** and do not have child safety certification.

### Can I wear the sun hat while hiking?

Yes — it has a chin cord with a breakaway clip, a moisture-wicking sweatband, and weighs about 90 g.

### Do you offer custom colors or embroidery?

Not currently. We plan to offer embroidery later in 2026; until then, all hats are final sale and not customizable.

## Sourcing and ethics

### Where are the hats made?

Designed in Arizona, manufactured in China under our audit. We can provide a supplier code of conduct on request for business buyers.

We are a DTC brand. For wholesale or bulk orders (100+ units), email us at wholesale@wanderbrim.com — we will route you to the right person. The live assistant collects your company name, quantity, and country, but a human handles the quote.
