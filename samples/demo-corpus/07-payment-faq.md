# Payment & Billing FAQ

## What payment methods do you accept?

- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- For EU orders: Klarna (pay later, 30 days)

We do not accept bank transfers, checks, or cash.

## My card was declined — what do I do?

A decline usually has one of these causes:

1. **Insufficient funds or card limit.** Check with your bank.
2. **3D Secure / verification.** Your bank may have sent a confirmation code to your phone — complete it and try again.
3. **Billing address mismatch.** The billing address must match the address your bank has on file.
4. **International or online purchases blocked.** Some banks block foreign/e-commerce transactions by default — ask them to allow it.
5. **Expired or entered incorrectly.** Double-check the card number and expiry date.

If the same card keeps failing, try PayPal or a different card. We do not see the reason your bank declined the charge — only that it was declined.

## I was charged twice

If you see two charges for the same order, do not panic:

- One may be a **pending authorization** that drops off on its own within 3–7 business days.
- If both are still on your statement after 7 days, forward your bank statement to support@wanderbrim.com with your order number. We will refund the duplicate.

## I haven't received an order confirmation

- Check your spam or promotions folder.
- Confirmations are sent from **orders@wanderbrim.com**.
- If it's been more than 1 hour and you can't find it, email us with the email address you checked out with. We can resend it.

## Can I change my payment method after ordering?

No — once an order is placed, the payment method is locked. If you want to switch cards, cancel the order (see Order Modification policy) and place a new one before it ships.

## Billing security

We do not store your full card number. Payments are processed through our payment provider (Stripe / PayPal) under PCI-DSS compliance. We will never ask you for your full card number by email or chat.

## Payments and disputes

If you do not recognize a charge, email us first before opening a dispute with your bank or PayPal. Most issues are resolved within one business day, and opening a dispute delays your refund.
