# Size Guide

## How to measure your head

Use a soft measuring tape. Wrap it around your head **about 1 cm above your eyebrows and ears**, where a hat would naturally sit. Keep it level and not too tight.

If you don't have a tape, use a piece of string and then measure it against a ruler.

## Size chart

| Size | Head circumference | Recommended for |
|---|---|---|
| S/M | 54 – 57 cm | XS–S clothing; most women |
| L/XL | 58 – 61 cm | M–L clothing; most men |
| XXL | 62 – 64 cm | XL and above |

## If you are between sizes

- For the **Sun Hat** and **Bucket Hat**, choose the larger size if you are between two sizes — the rear toggle lets you cinch it down.
- For the **Baseball Cap**, the snapback covers 54–61 cm, so between sizes is not an issue.
- For the **Beanie**, it is stretch-fit; size is not an issue.
